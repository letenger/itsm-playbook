# IT Support Onboarding Plan (30/60/90 Days)

Owner: Service Desk Manager. Applies to new Tier 1 service desk and IT support staff. Progress is reviewed weekly; each phase ends with a documented check-in. All names and systems below refer to the fictional Example University environment.

## First 30 days — Learn the service, shadow the work

Access and tooling:

- [ ] Accounts provisioned: Entra ID, M365, ITSM tool (ServiceNow), remote support tooling, phone/queue softphone
- [ ] Completed mandatory security awareness training and identity verification standard briefing
- [ ] Completed ITIL 4 Foundation e-learning (or scheduled with date recorded)
- [ ] Read `docs/service-catalogue.md`, `docs/incident-management.md`, and all three `runbooks/`

Knowledge and practice:

- [ ] Shadow at least 20 incidents across all ten catalogue services
- [ ] Handle 15 P4/P3 incidents under direct supervision
- [ ] Practise KEDB search before diagnosis on every shadowed ticket
- [ ] Observe one weekly CAB meeting (change visibility, not participation)

People:

- [ ] Meet each Tier 2 team lead (Identity, Endpoint, Network, AV, Applications, Security)
- [ ] Meet the Service Delivery Manager and understand escalation expectations
- [ ] Check-in at day 14 and day 30 with the Service Desk Manager

## Days 31–60 — Work with support, learn the systems

- [ ] Handle P3/P4 independently; Tier 2 escalation expected and reviewed, not penalised
- [ ] Complete the endpoint imaging process on three devices under observation
- [ ] Execute each runbook at least once in a training environment
- [ ] Write two knowledge base articles from real tickets (symptom, workaround, escalation)
- [ ] Shadow a Tier 2 rotation day in Network and one in Endpoint
- [ ] First KPI review: personal MTTA/MTTR against targets, first-contact resolution rate

## Days 61–90 — Own the work, contribute to the system

- [ ] Handle P2 incidents with escalation as needed; contribute to one P1 bridge as scribe
- [ ] Take a CAB shadow role: present one RFC as requester or reviewer
- [ ] Identify one recurring pain point and draft a KEDB entry or runbook improvement
- [ ] Complete a small improvement (knowledge article set, runbook update, or tracker refinement)
- [ ] 90-day review with Service Desk Manager: KPIs, knowledge contribution, and goals for the next quarter

## Sign-off criteria (day 90)

1. Meets MTTA/MTTR targets for P3/P4 across a full month.
2. Has executed all three runbooks at least once under observation.
3. Has contributed at least two knowledge articles or one runbook improvement.
4. Can explain the priority matrix, escalation paths, and change types without reference material.
